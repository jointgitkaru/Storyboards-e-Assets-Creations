## Full Animation Prompt Template

```
"Use the reference storyboard to make a full animation movie.
Audio: diegetic sound only — natural ambience, environmental foley, and subject-driven sound. No text.

Animate the fifteen panels of the reference storyboard as one continuous sequence, in order,
panel 1 through panel 15. Each panel is one second of screen time. Total runtime 15 seconds.

SUBJECT LOCK: [Paste the full SUBJECT description from the storyboard prompt, word for word.]
The subject must remain identical throughout.

STYLE LOCK: [Paste the STYLE, LIGHTING, and COLOUR GRADE from the storyboard prompt, word for word.]
Identical across all shots.

MOTION: [What moves in each beat and how — specific gestures, subject movement, environmental motion.
Continuous and cinematic.]

CAMERA: [Camera behaviour per shot, matching the framing established in each panel, smooth and cinematic.
One clear continuous flow.]

AUDIO: Diegetic sound only — natural ambience, environmental foley, and subject-driven sound
appropriate to this sequence.

[Aspect ratio], 15s, high resolution.

NEGATIVE: no subject drift, no colour drift, no design changes between shots, no morphing,
no teleporting objects, no panel numbers, no timecodes, no captions, no storyboard chrome
carried into the moving footage."
```

> **Note:** The chrome on the sheet is reference scaffolding, not content. The animation renders the panel imagery only. Never let the panel numbers, timecodes or caption strip appear in the moving footage.
